# Pokémon: Bond of Éire — Story & Feature Bible v3.0

## Vision Statement
Bond of Éire is a GBA ROM hack built on pokeemerald‑expansion. At its core it is a Pokémon game, but its heart asks a different question: *What does it mean to be truly trusted by something wild?* Every mechanic, every story beat, every line of dialogue serves that question.

## Core Theme
**“A bond forged in trust needs no cage.”**

## Core Mechanic: The Echo Bond System
A visible, per‑Pokémon bond rank from 1 to 10, displayed through text strings in the PokéNav’s repurposed Condition screen. No custom UI.

### How Bond Grows
- Winning battles side by side
- Using the Campfire System (once per in‑game day)
- Completing story events involving the Pokémon
- Walking together as a follower
- Restoring bond after Severance attacks

### How Bond Decreases
- Repeated fainting without healing
- Leaving a Pokémon boxed for long periods
- Certain story choices
- Team Fásach Severance attacks (temporary, with recovery after)

### Bond Rank Table
| Rank | Bond String (example) | Effect |
|------|----------------------|--------|
| 1‑2  | “Rockruff doesn’t know you yet.” | None |
| 3‑4  | “Rockruff is starting to get used to you.” | +5% all stats |
| 5‑6  | “Rockruff trusts you.” | Echo Bond unlocked |
| 7‑8  | “Rockruff would follow you anywhere.” | Echo + full Severance resistance |
| 9‑10 | “Rockruff looks at you with absolute trust.” | Max Echo + unique animations |

### The Echo Bond (Battle)
Once per battle, a Pokémon at rank 5+ can release a powered‑up Echo of its last used move when HP drops below 30%, or manually with a Bond Stone held item. 1.5× power. Brief warm flash, glowing eyes, afterimage silhouette.

### The Campfire System
Camping Gear key item received from Mum. Warps to a small campfire map. Lead Pokémon is already there, sitting by the fire. Interact for a bond‑reactive animation and +1 bond. Once per in‑game day. No dialogue, no tutorial — just fire, night, and Pokémon.

### The Severance Mechanic
Team Fásach admins and machines use Severance attacks in battle, temporarily lowering bond by 1‑2 ranks. The bond string changes mid‑battle (“Rockruff seems confused…”). After battle, bond recovers within a few steps.

### PokéNav Bond Display
The Condition screen shows the bond string plus a short environmental detail. Example at rank 9: *“Lycanroc sniffs the sea air and presses against your leg. It howls once, low and soft, at the stars.”*

## The World of Éire
Ireland‑inspired region of sea cliffs, bogs, stone circles, and mountains. Weather is a living system: rain, fog, rare golden sun, and story‑critical storms. Poké Balls require a wild Pokémon’s consent. Team Fásach uses Severance Balls that bypass consent — they claim to liberate, but enslave without agreement.

## Characters
- **Ciarán O’Cadhain** — Protagonist, 16. Quiet, stubborn, searching for his missing father.
- **Declan Ó Flaitheartaigh** — Rival. Bitter son of a farmer ruined by a flood Rowan predicted but couldn’t prevent. His team evolves from aggressive to supportive as his arc unfolds.
- **Mór‑Rí** — Antagonist. Rowan’s former student, whose partner Gardevoir died during a bond experiment. Believes bonds are traps; uses Severance Balls.
- **Rowan O’Cadhain** — Ciarán’s father, missing professor. Has been protecting the legendary stag Sliabhán for years.

## The Trials (5 Major Gym Leaders)
1. **Máire (Grass)** — Ballyduff. Herbalist. Heals your team before battle. Trial: navigate a bog maze, helping injured wild Pokémon.
2. **Cian (Water)** — Port Ceol. Fisherman. Trial: rescue fishermen during a scripted storm.
3. **Orlaith (Rock)** — Dunrock City. Stonemason. Trial: patience puzzle in an ancient quarry.
4. **Maeve (Psychic)** — Cahermoth Ruins. Mystic. Trial: three first‑person vision fragments of past events.
5. **Niamh (Fairy)** — Mallowdale. Festival organiser. Trial: restore a festival under siege by Team Fásach.

## Full Story Synopsis
- **Act I – The Vanishing:** Ciarán finds his father’s Pokédex and a farewell letter. Rockruff approaches him on the cliff — no battle, no Ball. Mum’s blessing. First rival battle with Declan. First encounter with Team Fásach.
- **Act II – The Search:** Ciarán follows Sliabhán’s trail through the five trials. Declan is forced into cooperation after a Fásach trap. Clues reveal Rowan is alive, protecting Sliabhán on the mountain.
- **Act III – The Summit:** Ciarán finds Rowan on Sliabh Mór. Mór‑Rí triggers a region‑wide Severance machine. The final battle is a bond test — only high‑bond Pokémon can fight. Sliabhán bows to Ciarán, allowing him to ride. The walk home.
- **Post‑Game – The Phantom Tide:** A phantom island (Hy‑Brasil) appears off the western coast. A short episode in which the player must restore a disturbed legendary sea creature’s bond through trust, not battle.

## Additional Features
- Following Pokémon with bond‑reactive animations
- Custom moves/abilities (Bond Link, Echo Surge)
- Weather affecting NPCs, routes, and wild encounters
- Infinite TMs, lore‑rich Pokédex, dynamic difficulty, bard NPCs, village restoration sidequests
